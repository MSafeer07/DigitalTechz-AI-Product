# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- A local browser app that builds a digital product in three clicks. Nothing is hosted, nothing is public.
- Click 1 finds five profitable pockets from live web research and scores them on the CLEAR test.
- Click 2 designs the product: before and after, a four-stage journey, price, chapters, and 15 to 20 research questions answered from real sources.
- Click 3 writes every chapter from designed blocks (takeaways, steps, flows, timelines, before and after, stats, examples, checklists) with sources cited, then packages the cover, introduction, store page, upsell, creator message and product charter.
- Three formats: ebook, video course (scripts), coaching offer (weekly programme).
- Optional pictures: a cover and one illustration per chapter in one style, on your own Gemini or OpenAI key.
- Exports: PDF, standalone HTML with pictures, Markdown. Products are saved in your browser.
